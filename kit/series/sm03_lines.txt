No solid ground. No flat land. No thick walls.
Just you, and four fights with gravity that you are not supposed to win.
Win all four, and congrats, you just beat gravity.
Level one. Pisa. The ground is soft clay, and your tower starts sinking on one side.
Level two. A knife-edge mountain. Two meters of rain a year turn the soil to mud, and mud slides.
Level three. Sydney. The roof is a sketch of sails, nobody knows how to build it, and some pieces weigh fifteen tonnes.
Final level. The harbor. A copper giant with skin as thin as two coins, and the storm wants to crumple her like foil.
Four levels, and nothing fell down. One tower still leans, but that's the famous part. You beat clay, mud, weight and wind, with lead, gravel, cable and iron.
