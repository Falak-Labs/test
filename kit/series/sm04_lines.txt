No pause. No rewind. No extra time.
Just you, and four fights with time that you are not supposed to win.
Win all four, and congrats, you just beat the clock.
Level one. London. Your clock has to strike right to the second, and wind, heat and cold all make it drift.
Level two. Still London. A tall ship is coming, your road is in its way, and nobody wants to wait.
Level three. A desert valley. Your skyscrapers are made of mud, mud melts in the rain, and they have to stand for five centuries.
Final level. China. Raiders on fast horses, mortar that crumbles in the rain, and a wall that has to last two thousand years.
Four levels, and you were never late. For once. You beat a lost second, a long wait, the rain and two thousand years, with pennies, water, mud and rice.
