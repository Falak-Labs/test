# Review 2 of 3: aqeedah and fiqh of repentance (long01 script_v1)

**Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES.** The creed is sound and the message (don't despair, seek knowledge, leave bad company) is the lesson scholars take from this hadith. The script needs fixes for (a) the rights of people, (b) two places where it does not match the hadith text, and (c) the wording about the monk.

1. **Rights of people (must fix).** Lines 49 and 64-65 make it sound as if everything is wiped clean. In the mainstream view, repentance takes care of Allah's right, but the victim's right still has to be paid back, either by the killer or by Allah making it up to the victim from His bounty (Ibn Taymiyya, Ibn Kathir): https://islamqa.info/ar/ref/219969. In our sharia, part of a killer's repentance is handing himself over to the victim's family: https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/147017. Add after line 70:
   > «بس انتبه.. التوبة الصادقة فيها شي مهم: إذا أخذت حق أحد.. رجّعه له، أو اطلب منه يسامحك. هذا من تمام التوبة.»

2. **Wrong detail (must fix).** Line 27 says he "asked for the most learned this time, not the most devout." In Muslim 2766 he asked for «أعلم أهل الأرض» both times, and line 16 already says so. Replace with:
   > «مئة. وما زال يسأل: مين أعلم أهل الأرض؟ وهذي المرة.. دلّوه على رجل عالم.»

3. **The monk (soften).** «يعرف يصلي كثير.. ويعرف يرحم قليل» (line 21) blames his heart. The scholars' point is that he gave a fatwa without knowledge, and that knowledge is better than worship without knowledge. Replace with:
   > «عنده عبادة كثير.. بس علمه قليل. وتكلم في شي ما يعرفه.»

   Line 19: add «من الأمم اللي قبلنا» after «راهب» so it doesn't read as a jab at a living religion. Line 23 «أخطر شي.. مو الذنب» puts despair above killing 99 people. Change it to:
   > «ومن أخطر الأشياء.. الشخص اللي يقول لك: ما فيه أمل.»

4. **Line 71.** As written, viewers could aim this at any imam who warns them. Replace with:
   > «واللي يقول لك: ما لك توبة.. يتكلم بغير علم. باب التوبة مفتوح.. لين تطلع الشمس من مغربها، أو توصل الروح الحلقوم.»

   This also gives the sermon the fear side it needs: a real deadline.

5. **«ما عنده أعمال» (lines 43, 64).** "He never did any good" is what the angels of punishment said. The angels of mercy said «جاء تائباً مقبلاً بقلبه»: his repentance and his walking were deeds. Line 43 «ما عمل شي» should become:
   > «ما عمل شي من اللي كان ناوي يسويه.»

   Line 64 should become:
   > «الرجل ما لحق يصلي ولا يتصدق. بس كان عنده أعظم شي: توبة صادقة.. وخطوة.»

   Line 45 should match the text: «جاء تائباً مقبلاً بقلبه إلى الله».

6. **Line 47 is ambiguous.** It should be:
   > «وأوحى الله للأرض الصالحة: تقرّبي. وللأرض الثانية: تباعدي.»

7. **Second hadith.** «مو ولدها» (line 56) is a guess, and it contradicts line 60 and the Prophet's words «طارحةً ولدَها». Use:
   > «ضايع منها ولدها.. وكل ما لقت طفل ضمّته وأرضعته.»

   Line 58: remove «والله» from the Companions' quoted answer, because the narration has «لا، وهي تقدر على أن لا تطرحه».

8. **«الله أرحم بك من أمك» (line 60).** The meaning holds a fortiori, and preachers use it, but don't present it as the wording of the hadith. Use:
   > «يعني رحمة الله بك.. أكبر من رحمة هذي الأم بولدها. وأكبر من رحمة أي أم.»

9. **Lines 33, 39, 65, 49: fine.** «مين يملك يقفل باب فتحه الله؟» is the scholar's own words explained. «والله قبله» and «فغفر الله له» come from the text (Bukhari 3470: «فغفر له»). «كل خطوة كانت توبة» is acceptable as rhetoric. «أخذ سيفه» and «فكر شوي» are harmless additions, but cut the sword.

10. **Angels.** Showing them as soft ambient light only, with no wings or figures, is the most cautious choice. For «ملَك على صورة إنسان», draw no human figure. Use narration over ambient light or text only, and never label one light as "the angel." Not depicting the Prophet ﷺ or the Companions is correct. Keep human figures faceless line art (see https://islamqa.info/ar/71170 on animation).

11. **AI voice.** Narrating hadith meaning, and saying ﷺ, in an AI voice is not a problem in itself. The duty is accuracy: check every Fusha hadith line letter by letter, with tashkeel, and check the AI's pronunciation. Keeping the Quran as text only is the correct, cautious choice.

12. **Sectarian or fatwa risk.** None, once #3 and #4 are applied. The video gives no rulings beyond the rights line.

**Hope/fear balance.** Hope is far too heavy. Fixes #1 and #4 are enough to balance it.
